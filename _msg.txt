feat: zoom into the chart's data, and dim the rest on hover

Twelve of the thirty-five points sat inside 36-42 on the board and a tenth
of a cent on the axis, which is a chart nobody can read. It now keeps a
window in the data instead of narrowing the SVG viewBox: the axes are
recomputed for the window, the dots keep their radius, a label is placed
only when its point is in view, and the marks are clipped to the plot.

Wheel zooms around the cursor, drag pans (waiting four pixels, so a press
that does not move is still a hover), Reset and a double-click come home,
and the bar says so. Hovering a point dims the others, which is what makes
a cluster legible without zooming at all.

Verified by rendering the page headless in Chrome: zoomed into the cluster,
reset back to the whole plot, and zoomed after switching to linear.
